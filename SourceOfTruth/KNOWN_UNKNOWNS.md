# Known unknowns

These items require a deliberate decision or playtest evidence. Codex must not invent them.

## Product

- Final commercial title, logo, art, audio, and trailer presentation.
- Whether the campaign needs optional challenge lengths after the open-ended dream-camper loop is proven.
- How many locations, routes, campers, buyers, and event variants produce enough replay value.
- Whether social statistics or score comparison add value without accounts.
- Whether optional alcohol/adult-cannabis content is enabled by default, opt-in, replaced in some territories, or reserved for a separately rated mode.
- Target age rating and the exact language/visual limits for adult campground culture.

## Balance

- Final starting cash/debt after real playtests.
- Final weight, tank, tow-limit, route, repair, offer, demand, and information values.
- How often event choices should interrupt travel/work.
- How much market history players can use without creating spreadsheet work.
- Whether technician availability variation is enjoyable or merely obstructive.

## Business and hosting

- Donation destination; no button is implemented until Anthony selects one.
- Public release/open-source timing.
- Whether accounts, cloud portability, telemetry, moderation, or anti-abuse controls will ever be needed.
- Hosting capacity and retention policy if playtest volume grows.

## Full mode and Unity

- Which subsystem families players actually want in greater depth.
- Whether detailed weight/load placement is fun enough for Full mode.
- Whether a Unity version is justified and whether it calls the PHP API or reimplements rules.

## Evidence

- Exact semantics of every decompiled Underworld helper and random bound.
- Cross-browser and accessibility behavior outside the latest recorded Chromium checks.
- Current-release full resale and dream-ending browser proof.

None of these unknowns blocks the next Simple-mode P0/P1 work. Record a decision here or in the owning document before implementation when consequences are non-obvious.
