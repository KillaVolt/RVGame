# Full mode

Status: future design only. Full mode must not delay Simple-mode playtesting and must use a new ruleset or explicit new campaign.

## Subsystems

1. Chassis, frame, and hitch
2. Tires and wheels
3. Brakes and bearings
4. Suspension and running gear
5. Roof, body, seals, walls, and floor
6. 12V distribution
7. 120V shore power and distribution
8. Battery and charging
9. Propane
10. Fresh water, grey water, black water, and plumbing
11. Appliances
12. HVAC
13. Interior fixtures and cabinetry
14. Exterior and presentation
15. Fictionalized safety equipment

Each subsystem may own actual wear, player evidence, defects, service history, installed components, and repair quality. Do not add every counter to every subsystem.

## Wear and maintenance

Distance is meaningful only where mechanically justified. Initial fictional balance candidates:

| Trigger | Game effect |
|---|---|
| Tires every 2,000 km since service | Add a wear band and increase eligible event weight |
| Bearings at 3,000 km | Make service-due evidence discoverable |
| Brakes at 4,000 km | Set service-due; unresolved use adds wear |
| Running gear every 5,000 km | Apply an archetype-weighted wear check |
| Tow vehicle at 5,000 km | Add operating-cost and breakdown-risk modifier |

These are game values, not real intervals. Roofs, seals, batteries, plumbing, propane, appliances, HVAC, and presentation should use days, cycles, weather, age, or events instead of fake mileage links.

Repairs reset only counters they explicitly own. Tire replacement does not reset bearings. Cosmetic work does not affect propane.

## Weight depth

Full mode may add hitch/tongue load, tow-vehicle payload, axle distribution, tank placement, consumable parts, and cargo location only after the Simple aggregate mechanic is proven fun and understandable. Values remain fictionalized and must not claim real towing safety.

## Knowledge and work

Full mode may add learnable skill, symptoms, tests, multiple plausible causes, parts inventory, specialized tools, work units, technician scheduling, repair quality, verification, and rework. A player without repair knowledge must still understand costs, uncertainty, and consequences without receiving hazardous real-world instructions.

## Optional adult lifestyle content

Alcohol and adult cannabis may appear later as optional campground/social content. Treat them as ordinary adult choices with time, spending, social, reputation, and next-day consequences. They are never required for progression, never marketed to minors, and never compatible with driving or towing during impairment. Support disabling or substituting this content if audience ratings, territories, hosts, or player preferences require it.

## Portability

A future Unity client may reuse documented IDs, plain-data state concepts, API behavior, and golden fixtures. React, PHP, and SQL do not automatically port. A reimplemented engine requires cross-language golden tests before save or score compatibility is claimed.
